Generated content (MIWI PDFs, logs, on-demand DB backups, PTW/IC attachment folders) is
NOT under server/ or anywhere in this repo — it lives in paths.DATA_DIR, an OS-appropriate
per-machine directory outside the repo by default (override with the PTW_DATA_DIR env
var): %LOCALAPPDATA%\PTW\server on Windows, ~/.local/share/ptw-server on Linux.
